# Worksheet

## Title

    15-minute workshop planning sheet

## Audience

    Independent tutors and workshop facilitators. A blank example to adapt to your own topic.

## Instructions

    The 3+9+3 minute schedule is an editable example. Replace the prompts or write your plan in the blank space. Before sharing, check the questions, timing and print layout for your group. Keep names and other personal information out of this sheet.
